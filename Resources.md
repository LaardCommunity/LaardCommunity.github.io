## Useful online tools

[Lipid Maps](https://lipidmaps.org/resources/lipid_web?page=index.html) – a free resource for lipid analysis. This site
provides an introduction to the chemistry and biochemistry of individual lipid classes, together with a compendium of
information on mass spectrometry of fatty acids.

[OpenChrom®](https://www.openchrom.net/) – an open source software for chromatography, spectrometry and spectroscopy.
Data from different systems can be imported and analysed, making this useful vendor-independent software.
